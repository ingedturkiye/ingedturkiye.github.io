Professional Development

A free online Zoom seminar series and a quarterly newsletter — supporting English language educators since 1995.

Zoom Seminar Series

A free online seminar series open to all educators — members and non-members alike. Sessions cover a wide range of ELT topics with live Q&A and interaction.

Online  ·  Free to attend  ·  Open to all educators

Quarterly Newsletter

The INGED Quarterly Newsletter is freely available to all educators. It includes articles by practitioners and researchers, conference and event news, ELTER research summaries, and professional resources.

4 issues per year  ·  Free to access  ·  Digital format
