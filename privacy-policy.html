Privacy Policy

How INGED collects, uses, and protects your personal data.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who We Are

INGED — İngilizce Eğitimi Derneği (English Language Teachers' Association) is a non-profit professional association registered in Turkey, headquartered in Ankara. This privacy policy explains how we handle personal data collected through our website (inged.org.tr) and our activities.

For any privacy-related questions, you can contact us at ingedturkiye@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following personal data when you interact with us:

  • Contact enquiries: name, email address, and any information you include in your message.
  • Membership applications: name, email address, institutional affiliation, and payment details processed through our designated payment channels.
  • Conference registration: name, email address, institutional affiliation, registration category, and bank transfer receipts submitted via our registration form.
  • Newsletter sign-ups: email address.

Our website does not use tracking cookies or analytics services that collect personal data beyond standard server logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data only for the purposes for which it was provided:

  • To respond to your contact enquiries.
  • To process membership applications and maintain membership records.
  • To manage conference registrations, verify payments, and communicate conference-related information.
  • To send newsletters and updates to members and subscribers who have opted in.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by applicable Turkish law. Membership and conference records may be retained for up to five years for administrative and legal compliance purposes.

5. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal retention obligations.
  • Withdraw consent to communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at ingedturkiye@gmail.com.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not store personal data on its servers. Data submitted through forms is handled via Google Forms and stored in Google's secure infrastructure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review it periodically.
