International Reach

INGED on the World Stage

INGED's partnerships with major international institutions reflect the association's standing as a trusted and credible voice in English language education. These collaborations bring international expertise into the Turkish ELT context and connect Turkish educators with global networks.

Our current flagship partnership is with the British Council, through which we run joint training and professional development programmes for English language teachers across Turkey. Our international affiliations with IATEFL and TESOL further reinforce our position in the global ELT community.

Technology-Enhanced Lesson Planning for 21st-Century Skills

A year-long professional development and co-authoring project. 60 selected teachers complete an online training cycle and writing workshops to produce an open-access handbook on smart lesson planning and 21st-century skills, with a national showcase in Bursa and three regional training days.
