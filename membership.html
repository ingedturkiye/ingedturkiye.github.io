How to Apply

Required Documents

To complete your membership application, send the following documents to ingedturkiye@gmail.com along with your bank transfer receipt:

1
Bank transfer (deposit) slip Proof of payment to the INGED bank account. Include your full name and "membership" in the description.
2
Turkish National ID (both sides) A clear scan or photograph of the front and back of your Turkish National ID card.
3
Passport-size photograph One recent passport-size photograph (digital scan accepted).

Student membership applicants should also include proof of current enrolment (e.g. a student certificate from their institution).

Once you have submitted the membership form, please send the required documents to ingedturkiye@gmail.com to complete your application.
FAQs

Membership Questions

When does my membership run from? +
Membership runs for one calendar year from the date your application is confirmed. You will receive a confirmation email from INGED once your documents and payment have been processed.
Can I join at any time of year? +
Yes. Membership applications are accepted throughout the year. If you are planning to attend the INGED Conference, it is worth joining before you register, as members receive a discounted conference registration rate.
How do I renew my membership? +
Use the Membership Renewal Form to submit your renewal. Pay the annual fee by bank transfer and send your updated deposit slip to ingedturkiye@gmail.com. Returning members do not need to resubmit their ID or photograph unless specifically requested.
Can I join if I am not based in Turkey? +
INGED membership is primarily for educators based in Turkey. If you are an international educator with an interest in Turkish ELT or a connection to INGED's work, please contact us to discuss your situation.
How do I access the reduced IATEFL or TESOL membership rate? +
Once your INGED membership is confirmed, contact us at ingedturkiye@gmail.com for details on how to apply for the reduced IATEFL or TESOL International membership rates available to INGED members.
