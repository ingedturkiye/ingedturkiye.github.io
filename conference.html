About the Conference

INGED International ELT Conference

The INGED International ELT Conference is held biennially and is one of the most important professional gatherings for English language educators in Turkey. The 23rd edition will take place at Tokat Gaziosmanpaşa University over three days, bringing together classroom teachers, teacher educators, researchers, and education policy professionals for plenary sessions, paper presentations, workshops, and professional networking.

Participants come from across Turkey and from international partner institutions. The conference provides a platform for sharing research findings, classroom innovations, and professional experience in all areas of English language teaching and learning.
