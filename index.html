About INGED

Turkey's Professional ELT Association Since 1995

INGED — İngilizce Eğitimi Derneği — was founded in Ankara in 1995 by a group of dedicated English language educators committed to improving the quality of English language teaching in Turkey. We are a non-profit, non-political professional association.

Our members include primary, secondary, and university-level English teachers, teacher educators, researchers, and education professionals from across Turkey. We are proud members of both IATEFL and TESOL International Association.

Mission

To promote professional development, research, and collaboration among English language educators in Turkey and internationally.
