Who We Are

About the Association

INGED — İngilizce Eğitimi Derneği (English Language Education Association) — was established in Ankara in 1995. We are a non-profit, non-political professional association serving English language educators at all levels across Turkey.

Our membership includes English teachers from primary, secondary, and tertiary education, as well as teacher educators, researchers, and education professionals with an interest in English language teaching and learning.

INGED is a member of both IATEFL (International Association of Teachers of English as a Foreign Language) and TESOL International Association, and maintains partnerships with the British Council Turkey, the European Commission's Erasmus+ programme, and Turkey's Ministry of National Education.

Our Work

Association Aims

INGED works towards its mission through a broad range of activities and programmes. Our core aims guide everything we do as an association.

1
Promote professional development for English language teachers at all levels of education in Turkey.
2
Organise and host the biennial International ELT Conference, bringing together educators from Turkey and around the world.
3
Support practitioner research through the ELTER Research Group and provide platforms for dissemination.
4
Foster a love of English and confidence in language use among young learners through the annual English Drama Festivals.
5
Establish and maintain partnerships with leading international organisations including IATEFL, TESOL, the British Council, and EU bodies.
6
Publish the INGED Quarterly Newsletter and maintain an up-to-date professional resource for members.

Vision, Mission & Benefits

Our Vision

"INGED strongly believes that our country needs more qualified English teachers who view themselves as professionals and who can educate citizens of high standards and expectations."

English is the global lingua franca — and Turkey needs educators who can meet that challenge. Multilingualism enriches individuals and strengthens societies: socially, economically, and culturally.

Our Mission

Improve the quality of English language teaching by creating platforms for sharing opinions, experiences, and knowledge. We provide teachers with:
